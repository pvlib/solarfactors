Bug fixes
---------
* The horizon band irradiance is now reduced by the obstruction of the
  neighboring PV rows on the front surfaces of the PV rows, in the same way
  as it already was on the back surfaces. Previously, the front surfaces
  always received the full, unobstructed horizon band. This changes results
  of :py:class:`~pvfactors.irradiance.models.HybridPerezOrdered`: front-side
  incident irradiance decreases for PV rows with a neighboring row on the
  side their front surface faces, and the effect grows with the row tilt
  (it is largest for vertical PV rows). (:issue:`14`, :pull:`XX`)
